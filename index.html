President's Message

2026-2027 Season


The Secret to Finding Great Fossils? Get Out There!

Everybody thinks you have to belong to an elite clique of fossil hunters or know about some secret hunting spot to find great fossils. Nothing could be further from the truth! The real secret to finding great specimens is simple: YOU HAVE TO GO!

You often see fossil hunters featured on the cover and inside the pages of this publication showing off some amazing finds. What's the common denominator among many of these successful hunters? They're getting off the couch, making plans, and heading out into the field!

I understand that not everyone can go fossil hunting every weekend. Life gets busy, and we all have other responsibilities. But if you only make it out once or twice a year, your chances of finding a whole bunch of great specimens are pretty slim. Most of you can probably manage to plan a trip once a month though, right? Now is the perfect time to start making those plans!

While our rivers are still a little too deep for comfortable fossil hunting, we should begin seeing the rains ease up and conditions return to what we typically expect here in Central Florida during the fall and winter. Over the next few months, river levels should drop, and fossil hunting season will be back in full swing.

Don't wait until the perfect conditions arrive to start planning. Get those trips on your calendar now! Check out the field trip pages in the Chronicles, and visit our Facebook page to connect with other hunters who are just as eager to get back out there. You never know what might be waiting for you beneath the river's surface!

FossilFest 2027: It's Time to Get Ready!

It's only November, but people are already talking about FossilFest 2027! In just a month or so, we'll begin signing up volunteers for TBFC's big weekend at the Florida State Fairgrounds. Mark these dates on your calendar: March 19, 20, and 21, 2027! FossilFest setup takes place on Friday, March 19, so start thinking about how you can help make our next big show a success.

FossilFest takes plenty of planning and a lot of helping hands. Whether you're a longtime volunteer or someone thinking about getting involved for the first time, there's a place for you at the show. Be ready!

Be good to one another and stay safe out there.

Good Hunting,
Mike Searle

Please be very cautious while venturing out into the rivers and creeks. There’s nothing in those rivers worth dying for.

New Members! - Over the last few meetings, we’ve been thrilled to welcome an increasing number of new members. It’s genuinely exciting to see fresh faces in the crowd, and we look forward to getting to know each of you, both at our meetings and out in the field. If you’re new to TBFC, thank you for choosing to join us. You are now part of a community of almost 600 fossil enthusiasts who share a passion for discovery, science, and Florida’s rich prehistoric past.

We meet monthly (except July and August), typically on the first Saturday. Schedule updates to the website will be made, as occasional schedule changes do occur. Field trips are also listed here and discussed at each meeting. We encourage you to attend regularly and join us afterward for a late dinner, where the conversation continues, usually about fossils, but not always, until the restaurant finally asks us to leave.

Announcements

Next Meeting Presentation

November 7, 2026
TBFC Annual Scholarship Auction with Dr. Bob Sinibaldi
7PM IN-PERSON CLUB MEETING at USF Behavioral Science Room #103

Looking for something special for the fossil hunter in your life? Maybe you're hoping to add another spectacular specimen to your own collection. Either way, this is your chance to pick up all sorts of great paleo-related goodies while supporting a very worthwhile cause. It's a win-win!

The auction will feature a variety of paleo treasures generously donated by club members and other supporters. So, if you have a few pesky megalodon teeth lying around or more mammoth molars than you know what to do with, why not donate one or two to the auction? Your contribution could help fund a student's education while finding a new home for a specimen someone will treasure.

Don't have any fossils you're willing to part with just yet? No problem! All you need is a big heart, an interest in paleontology, and perhaps a little room in your budget for some bidding. After all, the real secret to making the Ben Waller Memorial Scholarship Auction a success is YOU!

Once again, our very own auctioneer extraordinaire, Dr. Bob Sinibaldi, will be leading the festivities. With his booming voice, hilarious anecdotes, and entertaining auctioneering style, Dr. Bob knows how to keep things moving and the audience laughing. Even if you don't plan to bid on anything, the auction is a show in itself!

Of course, there's more to this event than great fossils and good laughs. The proceeds help support TBFC's operations and the numerous scholarships the club awards to students in geoscience programs at the University of South Florida and the University of Florida. These scholarships help support the next generation of scientists, making this an important event for the future of our field.

If you've never attended one of our auctions, I encourage you to join us on November 7. Dr. Bob and our generous donors make this a fun evening for everyone, whether you're an experienced collector, a beginning fossil hunter, or simply someone who enjoys a good time with fellow club members.

We'll have plenty of exciting paleo-related items up for grabs, including a good selection of legally collected artifacts. And here's another reason to come prepared to bid: TBFC accepts checks, credit cards, debit cards, and even gold doubloons! We want to make it as easy as possible for you to take home something special from the auction or our paleo store.
